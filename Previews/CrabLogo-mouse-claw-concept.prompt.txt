Initial concept prompt:
Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE refined original app logo for Crab, a macOS file utility. The user wants an ABSTRACT FUSION OF A COMPUTER MOUSE AND A CRAB CLAW with a LITTLE THREE-DIMENSIONAL DEPTH.
Design concept: ONE cohesive sculpted silhouette, a compact rounded wireless mouse whose front button region is itself an elegant open crab pincer. The low, plump rounded mouse body flows directly into TWO softly hooked claw tips at its upper end; the negative space between these tips is one deliberate tapered V-shaped pinch opening. Mouse and claw are inseparable, all formed from the SAME smooth shell. A tiny recessed scroll wheel and one restrained button seam convey the mouse identity. The asymmetric curved pincer tips convey the crab identity. Integrate these cues into a confident simple abstract brand symbol, NOT a crab holding a mouse and NOT a mouse with claws glued onto it.
View and form: near top-down, very mild three-quarter view just enough to show a narrow beveled sidewall. Orient the symbol on a slight elegant diagonal from lower left to upper right. Large softly rounded body, broad clean curves, precise negative-space opening, no small fussy details. Friendly compact proportions with a designed silhouette.
Material and color: warm vivid coral-orange/red shell, subtle tonal shading and a restrained soft edge highlight to give shallow 2.5D depth. Matte satin finish, no shiny plastic reflections or photorealism. Tiny darker inset scroll wheel. One soft short contact shadow. The dimensionality should support the geometry rather than overpower it.
Composition: one large symbol centered on a clean warm off-white square background with ample negative space, around 65 percent canvas occupancy. Premium modern macOS application identity, polished and instantly legible at small sizes.
Avoid: eyes, face, smile, crab body, crab legs, separate arms, antennae, wires, pointer-arrow shape, any literal cartoon animal, busy machinery, heavy 3D render, hyperreal hardware, gradients in the background, multiple objects, separate mouse plus separate claw, detailed illustration, text, letters, captions, watermark, presentation board, surrounding computer or mockup. Only the finished unified mouse-claw brand mark.
